Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your typical lead times?

Physical in-house 3D prototypes (PA-CF, PC, ABS) and laser-marked samples are ready in 24–48 hours. Full-scale production tooling typically takes 4–8 weeks depending on mold complexity (with rapid bridge tooling available in 10–15 days).

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

For in-house 3D printing and prototyping, our MOQ is 1 unit. For production plastic injection molding through our manufacturing network, our typical MOQ starts at 500 units per production run.

How does the DFM and prototyping process work?

First, our engineering team performs a thorough Design for Manufacturability (DFM) analysis on your CAD files. If needed, we build a precision in-house 3D prototype with laser-marked features for functional validation. Once form, fit, and function are approved, we coordinate tooling and injection molding production through our manufacturing network.
