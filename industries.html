Every industry imposes distinct thermal, chemical, mechanical, and regulatory criteria. We source tailored plastic injection molded solutions aligned with your compliance standards.

Domain Expertise

Sectors We Specialize In

Providing precision plastic manufacturing from JP Nagar, Bangalore to OEMs and innovators across India.

Automotive & Electric Mobility

Under-the-hood structural brackets, EV battery module spacers, wire harness connectors, dashboard bezels, and exterior trim.

Common Resins: PA66-GF30, PC-PBT, POM, ABS Heat-Resistant

Medical & Healthcare

Diagnostic monitor housings, surgical device handles, fluid collection cassettes, microfluidic chips, and cleanroom disposables.

Common Resins: Medical-grade Polycarbonate, USP Class VI PP, PMMA

Consumer Electronics & IoT

Handheld smart device casings, sensor enclosures, wearable watch bands, audio earbud shells, and snap-fit battery doors.

Common Resins: PC-ABS, Flame Retardant V0 Resins, TPU Overmolding

Packaging & FMCG

High-speed thin-wall food containers, tamper-evident beverage closures, flip-top cosmetic caps, and pharmaceutical vials.

Common Resins: Food-grade PP, HDPE, PET, Bio-based polymers

Industrial & Heavy Machinery

Low-friction gears, chemical pump impellers, wear plates, hydraulic manifold connectors, and protective motor cowlings.

Common Resins: POM (Delrin), PEEK, PTFE, Nylon 66-GF50

Household & Consumer Goods

Ergonomic cleaning tools, multi-tier drain baskets, collapsible utility crates, modern bathroom fixtures, and modular organizers.

Common Resins: High-impact PP, HIPS, SAN, Recycled PCR Resins

Warehousing & Logistics

Interlocking pallet tier separators, stackable transport totes, automated sorting guides, and heavy-duty shipping containers.

Common Resins: High-density Polyethylene (HDPE), Tough Impact PP
