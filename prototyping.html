De-Risk Your Manufacturing

Why Prototyping Before Mold Cutting is Essential

Injection molds represent a major capital investment. A single design flaw — such as an inadequate draft angle, incorrect snap fit, or improper clearance for PCB mounting — can cause expensive mold rework.

At Atelier Polytech, we de-risk your investment by delivering functional prototypes. Validate design before tooling: test assembly, pitch to stakeholders, and finalize mechanical dimensions before tool steel is cut. Production parts are injection molded.

  • 24–48 Hour Turnaround: Rapid turnaround on engineering prototypes without weeks of delay.
  • Direct DFM Feedback: Every prototype file is reviewed for injection mold compatibility within 24 business hours.
  • Flexible Quantities: From single functional proof-of-concept units to bridge pilot runs (injection molding starting at 500 units).

Production-Grade Material

CNC Plastic Machining

Subtractive machining from solid blocks of Delrin (POM), Nylon, PEEK, or PTFE. Delivers genuine production material properties and exceptional dimensional stability.

Tolerance: ±0.05mm • Lead time: 3–5 days
Low-Volume Production

Rapid Bridge Tooling

Aluminum or soft-steel single-cavity molds for pilot runs of 50 to 2,000 genuine injection-molded parts. Bridges the gap between 3D printing and full steel production tooling (4–8 weeks).

Volume: 50 – 2,000 pcs • Lead time: 10–15 days
