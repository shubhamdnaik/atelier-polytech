De-Risk Your Manufacturing

Why Prototyping Before Mold Cutting is Essential

Injection molds represent a serious capital investment. A single design flaw — such as an inadequate draft angle, incorrect snap fit, or improper clearance for PCB mounting — can cost hundreds of thousands of rupees in mold rework.

At Atelier Polytech, we de-risk your investment by delivering production-representative prototypes. You can test assembly, perform user studies, pitch to investors, and finalize mechanical dimensions before a single CNC tool touches mold steel.

  • 24–48 Hour Turnaround: Accelerate your design iterations without waiting weeks.
  • Direct DFM Feedback: Every prototype file is reviewed for injection mold compatibility.
  • Low MOQs: Support for single custom proof-of-concept pieces up to 500-unit pilot runs.

Our Technology Suite

Multiple Methods for Every Design Stage

Multi-Material & Composites

Bambu Lab H2S 3D Printing

Large 340×320×340 mm build envelope with 65°C actively heated chamber and 350°C hotend. Produces rigid, heat-resistant functional prototypes in Carbon Fiber Nylon (PA-CF), Polycarbonate (PC), ABS, ASA, PETG, and multi-color AMS configurations.

Build Volume: 340×320×340 mm • Lead time: 24-48 hours
Precision Marking & Cutting

10W Laser Engraving & Cutting

Equipped with an integrated 10W optical laser module and air assist. Direct-engraves permanent serial numbers, barcodes, QR codes, and logos on prototype enclosures; cleanly cuts acrylic panels up to 5mm, custom silicone gaskets, and faceplates.

Laser Power: 10W Optical • Lead time: 24 hours
Production-Grade Material

CNC Plastic Machining

Subtractive machining from solid blocks of Delrin (POM), Nylon, PEEK, or PTFE. Delivers genuine production material properties and exceptional dimensional stability.

Tolerance: ±0.03mm • Lead time: 3-5 days
Low-Volume Production

Rapid Bridge Tooling

Rapid aluminum or soft-steel single-cavity molds for pilot runs of 50 to 2,000 genuine injection-molded parts. Bridges the gap between 3D printing and full steel production tooling.

Volume: 50 - 2,000 pcs • Lead time: 10-15 days
