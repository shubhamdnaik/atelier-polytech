End-to-End Manufacturing Services

From initial design review and precision mold fabrication to automated injection molding and secondary assembly, we manage every engineering milestone.

Full Lifecycle Capabilities

Precision Engineering & Injection Molding

Everything required to take your plastic component from a digital 3D model to mass production at predictable unit economics and verified precision.

Plastic Component Sourcing

We match your part design with the ideal injection molding press in our vetted network. We manage raw material procurement, masterbatch coloring, and mold loading.

Whether you need 500 units for market testing or 1,000,000+ units for full-scale commercialization, our facilities scale seamlessly to meet your production ramp-up timeline.

Engineering Review & DFM

Comprehensive Design for Manufacturability (DFM) analysis: draft angle evaluation, wall thickness uniformity, gate locations, and parting line placement.

We identify potential mold issues before tool cutting begins, saving weeks of lead time and ensuring predictable cycle times.

Tooling & Mold Design

Design and fabrication of single-cavity prototype molds, multi-cavity production tooling, family molds, and hot-runner systems.

Built using tool steels (P20, H13, NAK80, S136) engineered for reliable shot lifespans. Tooling lead time is typically 4–8 weeks depending on mold complexity (bridge molds 10–15 days).

Rapid Prototyping (3D Printing & Laser Marking)

In-house engineering prototypes in 24–48 hours using heated-chamber FDM (PA-CF, PC, ABS) and 10W precision laser marking.

Validate form, fit, snap joints, and ergonomics before authorizing high-investment injection mold tooling. Production parts are injection molded.

Secondary Operations

Full post-molding finishing services: ultrasonic welding, threaded brass insert heat-staking, pad printing, laser etching, and conformal coating.

We deliver fully assembled sub-modules packaged and ready for your retail shelves or production line.

QA & Dimensional Inspection

Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) verification, optical comparators, drop testing, tensile loading, and color spectrophotometry.

Every production lot is released with a formal First Article Inspection Report (FAIR) and batch Certificate of Conformance.

Engineered Materials We Process

We regularly mold components using general-purpose, engineering-grade, and high-performance specialty polymers:

ABS & PC-ABS High impact resistance, consumer enclosures, automotive trim.
Polycarbonate (PC) Optical clarity, heat resistance, shatter-proof lenses, light guides.
Nylon (PA6 / PA66) Glass-filled (15-30% GF) structural components, high wear gears.
POM (Delrin / Acetal) Low friction, high stiffness, precision gears, mechanical latches.
Polypropylene (PP) Chemical resistance, living hinges, household containers, packaging.
TPE / TPU Elastomers Overmolding soft-touch grips, gaskets, flexible seals, shock mounts.

Our Workflow

From Design to Delivery in 5 Steps

A clear, transparent process designed to minimize risk and accelerate time to market.

01

CAD & DFM Review

Share STEP/IGES files. We review draft, wall thickness, and parting lines within 24 business hours.

02

Rapid Prototyping

Physical samples printed in 24–48 hours for form, fit, and ergonomic verification before tooling.

03

Mold Tooling

Hardened steel mold design and CNC/EDM machining (typically 4–8 weeks; bridge molds 10–15 days).

04

T1 Sample Approval

First article samples molded and measured. Client sign-off prior to volume batch run.

05

Production & Dispatch

Automated mass molding, secondary assembly, batch inspection, and direct delivery.
